01 — Overview

A teammate for contract review, not a replacement for one.

A conversational agent embedded directly in ClickUp. It answers partner contract questions and prepares revisions — without anyone leaving the task.

What it is

Operations writes one plain-English comment on a task. The assistant reads it, checks the partner's Service Order, and either answers the question or drafts the requested change.

Why it exists

Contract requests used to mean digging through folders, cross-checking Service Orders by hand, and waiting on manual edits. The assistant compresses that into a conversation — a human approver still decides what actually changes.

Note

The assistant is a support tool for Operations, not a replacement for judgment or approval authority. It prepares answers and draft revisions; the designated Commercial or Legal approver always decides before anything is finalized.

03 — Quick Start

Your first conversation, in three moves.

Before you begin, confirm four things on the task.

  • Task status is set to "Contract Review"
  • The customer's request is written in the task
  • The latest Service Order is available in the partner folder
  • The "Contract Review Agent" button has been clicked
Warning

If any of these four conditions is missing, the assistant will not respond as expected. Task status is the most common reason a conversation doesn't start — check that first.

04 — Requests

One clear comment. One hard rule.

Rule

Submit exactly one contract change per message. Requests containing multiple changes are stopped and must be resubmitted separately.

Accepted
"Please update the Monthly Platform Fee to EUR 1,250 per month."
Blocked
"Please update the address and change the Monthly Platform Fee."

Real request patterns

Structured value update
"Please update the Monthly Platform Fee to EUR 1,250 per month."
Company information
"Please update the registered office address to 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA, United Kingdom."
Legal clause revision
"Please revise the liability clause so that the Partner's aggregate liability is limited to the total fees paid during the previous twelve months."
  • Be specific — name the field, clause, or value that needs to change
  • Give context — mention who the customer is and what they're asking for
  • One change, one message — split multi-part requests before you submit

05 — Two Paths

Questions vs. revisions.

Every message you send takes one of two paths — intent detection decides automatically.

Message received→ Intent detection→ Path A or Path B
Path A · Question

Contract Question

  • Searches the approved knowledge base
  • Answers directly in the ClickUp comment
  • No approval required
  • No document is modified
Path B · Revision

Contract Revision

  • Prepares a draft of the requested change
  • Routes the draft to the correct approver
  • Waits for a Commercial or Legal decision
  • Edits the DOCX, uploads, and comments in ClickUp
Tip

You don't need to tell the assistant which path applies — intent detection decides automatically based on what you write.

07 — Approval Flow

Routing, then a human decision.

Once a message is identified as a revision, the assistant classifies it and routes it to the right approver automatically.

Approval classification→ Commercial or Legal approval→ Approval decision

Commercial approval

Fees · Pricing · Payment conditions · Company information · Operational Service Order fields

Legal approval

Liability · Governing law · Confidentiality · Compliance · Termination · Contractual wording

Note

Routing is automatic — you don't choose the approver. The assistant classifies the request based on what's being changed and sends it to the right team.

The AI prepares
It drafts the proposed revision and shows exactly what would change.
The approver decides
The designated Commercial or Legal approver reviews the proposed change and decides whether it may proceed.
Nothing changes without approval
No document is edited or published until approval is explicitly granted.
RoleResponsibility
OperationsInitiates the request and monitors the approval result
Commercial approverReviews and approves or rejects commercial changes (fees, pricing, company information)
Legal approverReviews and approves or rejects legal changes (liability, compliance, contractual wording)
Warning

Operations does not approve revisions. The role of Operations is to submit a clear, well-scoped request and monitor the outcome — the designated approver makes the decision.

Rule

Rejected approvals immediately stop the workflow. No partial changes are ever applied.

What happens after approval

Approval granted→ Retrieve Service Order→ Update DOCX→ Preserve formatting→ Publish to ClickUp
Note

Only the fields or clauses named in the approved request are changed. Everything else in the document — including formatting, headers, and unrelated clauses — is preserved exactly as it was.

08 — Live Example

One request, start to finish.

A realistic request followed through the entire lifecycle, so every piece above fits together.

1
Customer request
Acme Partners emails their account manager: "Can our monthly platform fee be updated to EUR 1,250? We agreed on this during renewal."
2
Operator action
The operator opens Task #4821, confirms status is "Contract Review," clicks "Contract Review Agent," and writes the request.
3
AI response
The assistant identifies the Monthly Platform Fee field and prepares a draft: current value EUR 950, proposed value EUR 1,250.
4
Approval
A commercial field, so it's routed to the Commercial approver, who reviews the side-by-side comparison and approves it.
5
Document generation
The assistant retrieves the latest Service Order, updates only the fee field, and preserves every other clause and all formatting.
6
ClickUp notification
A final comment confirms the update, with a link to the revised file in Acme Partners' Drive folder.
7
Completed task
The operator confirms the update and replies to the customer with the new fee, referencing the revised Service Order.
Tip

Notice that Operations never approves anything and never edits the document directly — the operator's job is steps 2 and 7 only.

09 — Habits

Best practices.

Habits that keep every conversation fast, accurate, and safe.

  • One change per message — mixing topics gets requests blocked
  • Provide complete customer information up front
  • Review every draft carefully, like a colleague's first draft
  • Let the right approver decide — never approve on their behalf
  • Avoid unrelated discussions in the task thread
Correct
"Please revise the liability clause so aggregate liability is capped at 12 months of fees."
Incorrect
"Please update the address and change the fee and fix the liability clause" — three changes in one message.

11 — Troubleshooting

Know the cause before you escalate.

User error

Request was vague, mixed changes, or missing information.
Fix: rewrite and resubmit.

Approval rejection

The approver reviewed and declined.
Fix: read the feedback, adjust, resubmit.

Missing documentation

Latest Service Order isn't in the partner folder.
Fix: upload it first.

Workflow issue

The assistant didn't start or respond as expected.
Fix: check the Quick Start checklist, then escalate.

IssueCauseAction
Assistant does not startTask is not in "Contract Review" statusUpdate the task status, then try again
Assistant does not start"Contract Review Agent" button was not clickedClick the button to activate the assistant
Revision cannot be generatedLatest Service Order is missing from the partner folderUpload it, then continue the conversation
Request is blockedMore than one change was requested in a single messageSubmit each change as a separate message
Approval workflow stopsThe designated approver rejected the draft — expected behaviorCorrect the request based on feedback and submit again
Document looks incorrectThe generated revision needs review before it's sharedDo not share it; reject it and report to the Product Team
Note

A rejected approval is not a technical error — it means the process worked correctly and the change did not meet the approver's criteria.

Glossary

Service Order — defines the commercial terms of a partner's active contract.

Contract Review — the ClickUp task status that makes a task eligible for the assistant.

Knowledge Base — the approved internal source used to answer questions.

Approval — the decision required before any document change is applied.

Approval Classification — determines if a revision is Commercial or Legal.

Revision — a single proposed change to a Service Order or clause.

Partner Folder — the Drive folder holding a partner's contract documents.

Intent Detection — determines if a message is a question or a revision.
